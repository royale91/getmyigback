Are you affiliated with Instagram or Meta?

No. This is an independent guide and service with no connection to, endorsement by, or affiliation with Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc. We just know how their front door and their legal door differ.

How long does it take?

Most cases that come back land in a 10–30 day window after the certified letters are delivered — so start checking your login around day 15. In the founder's own case it was eleven days. Some take longer, some never move; there's no set clock and no guarantee. Do not restart the appeal loop while you wait — the letter is doing the work.

No — and you should walk away from anyone who does. This documents a method that worked for a real account and has worked since. It will not undo a legitimate ban. See "who this is for" above.
